The orange one

Thambili, not the green kind.

King coconut is its own variety, native to Sri Lanka. Shorter tree, bright orange husk, and a water that arrives sweet without help.

It is what people there actually drink, opened in front of you at the roadside.

The ingredient list

One line long.

King coconut water. That is all of it. No added sugar, no preservatives, nothing reconstituted from concentrate somewhere else.

The bottle

Glass, on purpose.

Plastic ships cheaper and stacks easier. Glass means the water never sits against plastic on its way from Sri Lanka to your fridge.

350ml. Gold cap. Meant to be drunk cold.

The source

The Coconut Triangle.

The belt of coastal plain and plantation between Puttalam, Kurunegala and Gampaha, in Sri Lanka's north west. It grows more than half the country's coconuts.

Sourced, pasteurised and bottled there, so it leaves the island finished.

Everything on the label, and nothing that isn't.
