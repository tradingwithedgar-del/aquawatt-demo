What is the door doing?

What Carlos handles

Springs, openers, cables, new doors

Residential and commercial work, anywhere in Houston and the areas around it.

Spring replacement

Torsion and extension. The tension in a loaded spring is enough to break a wrist, so this is not a weekend job.

Opener repair and install

Chain, belt and screw drive. Sensor alignment, remotes, keypads, full replacements.

Cables, rollers, track

Snapped or frayed cables, worn rollers, bent track, doors that have jumped the track.

New door installation

Full replacement, residential and commercial, old door hauled off.

Tune-up and maintenance

Balance, lubrication, hardware, safety reverse check. Costs less than the spring it prevents.

Commercial doors

Shops, warehouses and rental property. Doors that have to open first thing Monday.
